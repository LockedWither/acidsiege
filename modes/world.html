You start as one nation among many bots. EXPAND (the green mode button) and click your border to claim neighbouring neutral land — more land means more income. To take an enemy nation you must first destroy its territory: pick a weapon and drop it directly on the country. Wiped land turns neutral, then you expand into it. Level up your nation to raise its 🛡 resistance so enemy (and bot) weapons can't carve you up. Bots expand and attack each other and you. Conquer 65% of the world — or be the last nation standing — to win.
